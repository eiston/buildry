Buildry

Privacy policy

Last updated October 22, 2026

Buildry helps people explore stays, book tours, manage accounts and tenancies, and sign leases. This policy explains the personal information handled through the Buildry website at app.buildry.ca. A separate Photo ID terms and privacy notice gives more detail about identity documents requested after lease signing.

Information we collect

  • Accounts: name, email address, phone number when provided, account role and status, and a password hash if you set a password.
  • Google sign-in: your Google account's unique identifier, verified email address, and given and family names when available. Google may show your profile picture on its consent screen; Buildry does not store that picture. Buildry does not request access to your Gmail, contacts, Drive, or calendar.
  • Tours and tenancies: contact details, booking preferences, property and room choices, lease details, signatures, and related communications and documents.
  • Identity documents: a government-issued photo ID is requested after signing a lease. The Photo ID notice explains its purpose, restricted access, and retention.
  • Site use: session cookies needed to keep you signed in, and technical information such as IP address and browser details that may be recorded when you sign a lease or use the service.

How we use it

We use this information to create and secure accounts, sign you in, respond to inquiries, arrange tours, prepare and administer leases, verify identity for a signed lease, support tenants and landlords, and operate the property catalog. Google sign-in information is used for authentication and account matching. It is not used for advertising.

Who receives it

Authorized Buildry staff can access information needed for their work. Service providers help operate the website and deliver email. When you choose Google sign-in, Google handles the sign-in step and sends Buildry the basic account information described above. We may disclose information when required by law or as needed to administer a tenancy.

Storage, security, and retention

Account and tenancy records are stored in Buildry's database. Sessions use signed, HTTP-only cookies. Uploaded photo ID images are encrypted and available only through staff-authorized routes; access is logged. We keep other records as needed for the purposes described above and applicable recordkeeping obligations. Photo ID images are retained during the tenancy and automatically deleted one year after Buildry records the tenancy as ended, unless preservation is required by law. Backups may persist through their normal backup cycle.

Your choices and requests

You can use an email and password instead of Google sign-in. You can disconnect Buildry from your Google Account settings; contact us if you also want us to review the Google link saved in your Buildry account. To ask about access, correction, or deletion of your information, email eiston.wei@gmail.com. Some tenancy or legal records may need to be retained.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page when the service or its privacy practices change. The date above shows the latest published version.
